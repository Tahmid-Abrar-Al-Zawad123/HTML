About My Character

Character Name: Gojo Satoru

Anime Series: Jujutsu Kaisen

Favourite Food: Sweet Things (Kikufuku Mochi)

Special Power: Limitless and Six Eyes

Why I Like This Character

Gojo is widely known as the strongest jujutsu sorcerer in the world.

He balances a playful, carefree attitude with an incredibly cool and serious fighting style.

He deeply cares for his students and fights to protect the future generation.
